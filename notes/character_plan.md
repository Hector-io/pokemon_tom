# Character, gym, and Elite Four plan

This document records intended replacements and creative direction. It does not
mean that a sprite, dialogue change, or team has already been implemented.

For each replacement, the usual work is: overworld sprite, trainer sprite and
back sprite where needed, trainer class/name, dialogue and cutscenes, battle
team, and music assignment.
